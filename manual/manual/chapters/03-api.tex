\chapter{API reference}
\label{ch:api}

This chapter lists the entry points a user is expected to reach for,
layer by layer.  It is a map, not a transcription: the authoritative
signature of every function is its docstring in the source, and the
module-level \texttt{\_\_all\_\_} lists are the canonical inventories.
Entries marked \emph{stable} have been exercised by the verification
records in Chapter~\ref{ch:verification}; the rest follow the same
conventions but have lighter coverage.

\section{policy: selection rules}

The policy layer turns analysis output into selections: composition-based
rules, entropy-based ordering, the ranked orbital selection with its
singly-occupied and virtual-removal options, and the verification
helpers.  The module names are the inventory
(\texttt{policy.\allowbreak composition}, \texttt{policy.\allowbreak entropy},
\texttt{policy.\allowbreak ranking}, \texttt{policy.\allowbreak rules}, and their siblings);
the docstrings state the conventions each rule follows.

The two factory families exist because of a pyscfad constraint: the
\texttt{Mole}-based factories cannot be traced inside
\texttt{jax.jit}, and the theta-only factories are built for exactly
that context.  When in doubt about which to call, ask whether the
geometry is fixed; if it is, use the theta-only factory.

\section{cli}

\texttt{python -m diffmrc.cli} runs an end-to-end calculation from a
geometry file; \texttt{--help} lists the options.  The command-line
entry is the least settled surface of the package.
