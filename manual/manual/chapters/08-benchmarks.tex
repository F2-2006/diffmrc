\chapter{Benchmarks}
\label{ch:benchmarks}

Two benchmark lines exercise the package end to end.  The first is a
ground-state line against direct PySCF runs at matched settings; the
second is the excited-state line against the QUEST reference set,
whose protocol and reading are walked through in the Tutorial
(Chapter on the benchmark walkthrough).  This chapter records both
with their conventions, so that a quoted number can be traced back to
its run.

\section{Ground-state line (M5-A)}

Three closed-shell molecules at matched geometry, basis, and active
window, chain against PySCF:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrl}
\toprule
System & Deviation (Ha) & Note \\
\midrule
LiH & $-3.5\times 10^{-12}$ & active window [4,5], 6-31G \\
HF & $-3.6\times 10^{-13}$ & active window [1,2], 6-31G \\
NH$_3$ & $-1.9\times 10^{-10}$ & active window [1,2], 6-31G \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The agreement is at the level where the two codes' convergence
tolerances meet.  The chain's end-to-end entry reports the energy and
the stationarity diagnostics; nuclear gradients on this line come
from the lower-level interface and are covered by the first-derivative
records of Chapter~\ref{ch:verification}.

\section{Excited-state line (QUEST subset)}

Fourteen vertical excitation energies over seven molecules, 6-31G,
the frontier-window protocol with its corrections, against the QUEST
database's best estimates and its own \textsc{sc}-NEVPT2 column.  The
full table sits in the Tutorial; the summary by family:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
Family & MAD vs best estimate & MAD vs \textsc{sc}-NEVPT2 \\
\midrule
$n \to \pi^*$ (8 states) & $0.31$~eV & $0.36$~eV \\
$\pi \to \pi^*$ (6 states) & $1.14$~eV & $1.08$~eV \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Two protocol facts belong to these numbers.  The basis is 6-31G while
the reference set's values are near the complete-basis limit, so part
of every deviation is basis, not method; and the window corrections
of the Tutorial's active-space chapter move individual entries by up
to a factor of two in deviation before the family statistics are
computed.  The residual $\pi\pi^*$ deviation --- uniform in sign
across five chemically different systems --- is recorded as a
capability boundary of the minimal-space protocol at this basis, not
as a molecule-specific error.

\section{Selection-protocol cross-check (DVS-APC)}

The entropy-based active-space selection layer was checked against a
published automated-selection data set on its own terms: a port of
the published formula evaluated on every orbital of every record
agrees with the published entropy values to exactly zero difference,
the energy layer reproduces reference state energies to
$10^{-11}$~Ha, and the protocol's singly-occupied rule is confirmed
on the full data set.  The cross-check also fixed a defect this
package had carried: the published protocol includes a
virtual-removal correction of order $N$ that the port initially
lacked; with the correction, the selection statistics move from the
high-0.5 range to the 0.6--0.7 range in agreement with the source.
When the two selections disagree, the record says so; the report of
that campaign is stored with the data.

\section{What is not benchmarked}

Large-active-space behaviour, GPU execution, spin--orbit coupled
targets, and relativistic Hamiltonians have no benchmark here because
they are outside the present scope
(Chapter~\ref{ch:limitations}).  A number produced by this package on
those targets has no verification record behind it.
